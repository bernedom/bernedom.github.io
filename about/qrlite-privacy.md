QRLite is an Android app provided by SoftwareCraft GmbH. The goal of the app is simple: to provide an AI-free QR code reader that respects your privacy and keeps scanning fully local on your device.

QRLite does not collect any personal information, usage data, location data, or any other user information. The app does not store your scans, does not read your contacts, and does not access personal files on your device for any purpose other than the QR scanning function itself.

QRLite does not transmit any information to the cloud, to our servers, or to any third-party provider. QR content is processed locally on your device and remains under your control. The app does not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or cloud-based AI processing.

The only data tracked is basic aggregated statistics about app downloads and installs. This is only used to understand how widely the app is being used and is not linked to any individual user. It is not used for profiling, advertising, or personalization.

SoftwareCraft GmbH does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

QRLite is designed around privacy by default. We believe that scanning a QR code should not require giving up your privacy, and we are committed to keeping the app simple, local, and respectful of your data.
